WHO WE ARE

About Fpag

Fpag is built around a simple idea: make online shopping easy to understand and easy to use.

Our Story

Fpag is an e-commerce web application created to provide customers with a straightforward online shopping experience.

Customers can browse available products, add products to their shopping cart, change quantities, review their order, and provide delivery information during checkout.

Behind the interface, the application uses server-side processing and a database to manage products, orders, users, and stock.
